Calendars

Private iCloud calendars (running as a Home Assistant app): first add iCloud to Home Assistant with its CalDAV integration (see the README), then tap + Home Assistant calendar and pick the calendar.

Public calendar link: on your iPhone, open the Calendar app, tap Calendars, then the ⓘ next to the calendar. Turn on Public Calendar, tap Share Link… and Copy, then tap + Calendar link and paste it. Anyone with that link can see the calendar.

Countdowns
